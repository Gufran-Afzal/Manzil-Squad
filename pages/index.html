How we run trips

We are new, so we won't ask you to take our word for anything. Here is what you can hold us to.

Read how we plan
Nothing is confirmed until it's in writing
Proposed trips are labelled proposed. You'll get the date, price, coach company and place we stay in writing before you pay a rupee.
The price you see is the price you pay
Each trip lists what is and isn't included. If something isn't on the written confirmation, it isn't included, and we'll say so up front.
Coordinators travel with every group
Headcounts at every stop, emergency contacts on paper, separate rooms for male and female participants, and a plan for bad weather.
We'd rather postpone than push on
If snow, rain or road conditions make the trip unsafe, we move the date. We check advisories before every departure.

How booking works

  1. 1

    Register interest

    Fill in a short form or message us on WhatsApp. It's free and doesn't commit you to anything.

  2. 2

    We confirm the details

    Once enough people are interested, we book the coach and the stay, then message you the final date, price and itinerary.

  3. 3

    You decide and pay

    If it suits you, pay by the method we share in writing. Your seat is confirmed when we confirm it to you in writing.

  4. 4

    Travel together

    A packing list and briefing before departure, then two days in the hills with your group.
