How we plan a trip

What happens between "we'd like to run this trip" and the coach leaving Lahore, written down so you can check we do it.

Transparent planning

Every trip on this site has a status. Registering interest means we're measuring demand. Tentative means a date is pencilled in. Booking open means dates, price and suppliers are confirmed. We never show countdown timers or "only 3 seats left" messages unless they're true.

Before we ask for any payment, you'll receive a written confirmation with the date, the final price per person, what's included and excluded, the coach company, where we'll stay, and the cancellation terms.

Checking suppliers

These are the checks we commit to making before we book a coach or accommodation. Each one is recorded so that we can show it if asked.

  1. Transport company. We ask for the company's registration details and the vehicle's documents, check that the vehicle is suitable for hill roads, and get the driver's name and contact number in advance.
  2. Accommodation. We confirm the property exists and is operating, get photos of the actual rooms, confirm room sharing and separate rooms for male and female participants, and get the booking in writing.
  3. Prices in writing. We get written quotes so that the price we give you is based on real costs, not guesses.
  4. A backup plan. We note an alternative coach and stay in case the first choice falls through.

Note

Tourism businesses in Pakistan are regulated by provincial authorities. We'll list our own registration here once we have it. You can read how to check any operator in our guide to choosing a safe tour operator.

Keeping you informed

  • When a trip's status changes, everyone who registered hears from us directly.
  • Before departure, you'll get the itinerary, packing list, pickup point and coordinator contacts.
  • Parents or guardians can contact the coordinators. We'll share the number with participants to pass on.
  • If plans change during the trip because of weather or roads, we explain why, on the spot.

Safety

Coordinators travel with the group, keep a headcount at every stop and carry a written list of emergency contacts. We check weather and road advisories before departure and postpone if conditions are unsafe. The safety page sets out what we do and what we ask of participants.

Responsible travel

Ayubia is a national park, and the Galiyat forests are under pressure from heavy visitor numbers. On our trips we carry litter out, stay on marked tracks, don't feed wildlife and keep noise down in the evenings. We prefer locally owned places to eat and stay when they meet our standards.
