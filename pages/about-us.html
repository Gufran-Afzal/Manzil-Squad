Trips we'd want to go on ourselves

We're a small team in Lahore starting a travel project for university students. We haven't run our first trip yet, and this page says so plainly.

Why this exists

Most students in Lahore want to see the north, and most first trips go one of two ways: a crowded, last-minute plan between friends, or a package found on social media with a price, a photo and very little else.

The second kind can be fine. It can also mean a different bus from the one advertised, a room for eight when you were promised four, or a refund that never arrives. When you are 19 and paying with money you saved, those things matter.

We want to run trips that are affordable for students and planned with the same care you'd want if your younger sibling were going. That means writing things down, saying what is and isn't confirmed, and being easy to reach.

Where we are now

We are pre-launch. Our first proposed trip is a weekend in Nathiagali and Ayubia. We're collecting registrations of interest to see whether there is enough demand, and we're checking transport and accommodation before we book anything.

We are not claiming to be licensed or insured until we can show you the paperwork. When we have it, it will be on this site with the registration number so you can check it yourself.

What we value

Honesty about what's confirmed
A proposal is labelled a proposal. A price range is labelled a range. We'd rather lose a booking than mislead someone into one.
Care for the people who come
Coordinators who stay with the group, sensible rooming, and parents who can reach us if they need to.
Respect for the places we visit
The Galiyat forests are protected areas and people's homes. We leave no litter, keep noise down and follow park rules.
Being reachable
A real person answers messages, before, during and after a trip.
