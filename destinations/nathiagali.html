About Nathiagali

Nathiagali sits in the middle of the Galiyat, a chain of small hill resorts along the ridge between Murree and Abbottabad. It is quieter and more forested than Murree, which makes it a good first trip to the hills.

Most visitors come for the forest walks, the views from the ridges and the cooler air. The town itself is small: a short bazaar, guest houses and hotels, and a wooden church from the colonial period.

What to see

Mushkpuri Top
At about 2,800 m, the second-highest peak in the Galiyat. The route from Nathiagali is a steady climb of about 4 km. Only for groups with good fitness and good weather.
Pipeline Track
A mostly level, shaded forest walk of about 4 km between Dunga Gali and Ayubia.
St. Matthew's Church
A wooden church from the colonial period on the edge of the ridge.
Miranjani
The highest peak in the Galiyat at about 2,992 m. A longer, harder hike that needs a local guide and an early start.

Seasons

Summer is cool and green; the monsoon brings near-daily rain from July to mid-September; winter brings cold and, in some years, heavy snow.

  • Winter runs roughly from November to February. Snowfall has become more common in February than in December or January.
  • Monsoon rain can make trails slippery and roads slow.
  • Weekends and public holidays can bring heavy traffic on the roads up from Murree.

Travel advice

  • Pack a warm layer in every season. Evenings are much colder than in Lahore.
  • Mobile signal varies between networks. Download offline maps before you leave.
  • Book accommodation directly with a property you can verify, or through an operator who names the property in writing.

Safety

  • Do not attempt Mushkpuri or Miranjani in fog, snow or late in the day.
  • Follow Pakistan Meteorological Department and local administration advisories during heavy snow.
  • In heavy snowfall, never stay in a parked car with the engine running: snow can block the exhaust.
