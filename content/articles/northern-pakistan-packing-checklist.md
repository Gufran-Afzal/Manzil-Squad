The most common mistake on a first trip north is packing for Lahore weather. The hills can be cool in summer and very cold at night, and the weather can change within an hour. Use this list as a starting point and adjust it for the season and the trip.

## Documents and money

- [ ] **Original CNIC** (or B-form for under-18s). Carry it on you, not in your main bag. There are security checkpoints on some routes, and hotels may ask for ID.
- [ ] **Student card**, if the trip or any entry ticket asks for it
- [ ] A **photo of your CNIC** on your phone, as a backup only
- [ ] **Cash** in small notes, kept in two places. Card machines and ATMs are less common in hill towns.
- [ ] Your **trip confirmation and reference number**
- [ ] The **coordinators' phone numbers** written on paper, not only saved in your phone

## Clothing for every season

Layers work better than one thick jacket, because you can add and remove them as you climb and as the day warms and cools.

- [ ] A **warm jacket or fleece**, even in June. Evenings at 2,400 metres are much colder than Lahore.
- [ ] A **light waterproof jacket** (essential in the monsoon, useful all year)
- [ ] **Comfortable walking shoes with grip.** Not new shoes: break them in first.
- [ ] Two pairs of **thick socks**, plus spares
- [ ] **Comfortable trousers** you can walk in
- [ ] A **cap or hat** for sun, and a warm hat for evenings
- [ ] **Sleepwear** suitable for cold rooms

### Extra for winter

- [ ] Thermal base layers
- [ ] Gloves and a scarf or neck warmer
- [ ] Waterproof boots if there is snow
- [ ] An extra warm layer for the coach

### Extra for the monsoon

- [ ] A small umbrella or poncho
- [ ] A quick-drying change of clothes
- [ ] Plastic bags to keep clothes and electronics dry
- [ ] Spare socks (wet socks ruin a walk)

## Health and comfort

- [ ] **Personal medicines**, with a copy of the prescription, in your hand luggage
- [ ] **Travel-sickness tablets** if you get sick on winding roads (hill roads are winding; ask a pharmacist what suits you)
- [ ] **Sunscreen and lip balm**: UV is stronger at altitude, even when it feels cool
- [ ] A small **hand sanitiser** and tissues
- [ ] Any **period products** you need, as shops in small towns may have limited choice
- [ ] Plasters for blisters

> **Safety:** If you have a medical condition that could matter on a long journey or a walk, tell the trip coordinators privately before departure. You don't need to put the details in any online form.

## Electronics

- [ ] A **power bank**, charged. Power cuts happen, and phones drain faster in the cold.
- [ ] Charging cable
- [ ] **Offline maps** of the area, downloaded before you leave
- [ ] A small **torch** or headlamp (phone torches drain the battery)

## For the journey

- [ ] A **reusable water bottle**
- [ ] Snacks for the road
- [ ] A neck pillow if the drive is long
- [ ] Earphones
- [ ] A small bag for rubbish. Carry your litter out of forests and parks.

## How to pack it

How you pack matters almost as much as what you pack.

- **One main bag, one small day bag.** The main bag stays on the coach or in the room; the day bag carries what you need on a walk.
- **Day bag contents:** water, a warm layer, rain jacket, snacks, power bank, cash, CNIC, any medicines, and a small rubbish bag.
- **Keep heavy items low and in the middle** of the bag, so it carries comfortably.
- **Pack a plastic bag inside** your main bag as a liner in the monsoon, so a wet coach roof or a rainy walk doesn't soak everything.
- **Label your bag** with your name and a phone number. On group trips, identical black bags get mixed up.

## What not to bring

- **Valuable jewellery** or anything you'd be upset to lose
- **Too much luggage.** Space on a coach is shared, and you may carry your bag up steps or slopes.
- **Drones**, unless you have checked the current rules. Drone use can be restricted in many areas.
- **Alcohol, drugs or weapons.** Not allowed on our trips, and illegal or restricted in many places.

> **Verify:** Rules on drones and on photographing certain sites can change and differ between provinces. Check current rules before you take a drone.

## A quick final check

The night before you leave, make sure you have your **CNIC, phone, power bank, warm layer, rain layer, medicines and cash**. If those seven are packed, almost anything else can be borrowed or bought on the way.
