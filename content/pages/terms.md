These are draft terms. They describe how registration and booking are intended to work and will be reviewed by a qualified professional before any payment is accepted.

## Registering interest

- Registering interest, through the website form or on WhatsApp, is free.
- It does not reserve a seat, create a booking or commit you to pay.
- It lets us estimate demand and contact you when details are confirmed.

## Proposed and confirmed trips

Each trip page shows a status. A trip marked *registering interest* or *tentative* is a proposal: its date, price, itinerary and suppliers can change or the trip may not run. A trip marked *booking open* has confirmed dates, a confirmed price and confirmed suppliers.

## When a booking is confirmed

A seat is confirmed only when **both** of these have happened:

1. we have received your payment by a method we gave you in writing, and
2. we have sent you a written confirmation (by WhatsApp message or email) that names you, the trip, the date and the amount paid.

A form submission, an automatic message or a screenshot of this website is not a booking confirmation.

## Price and what is included

The price per person and the list of inclusions and exclusions in your written confirmation are the ones that apply. If something is not listed as included, assume it is not included. We will not add charges after confirmation except for optional extras you choose to buy.
