Best way to reach you

We only use these details to organise the trip. We don't sell or share them, and we never ask for card details or passwords.

What happens next

  1. You get a reference number straight away. Keep it: it helps us find your request.
  2. We count the interest. The trip goes ahead only if enough people register.
  3. We confirm the details and message you with the final date, price and itinerary.
  4. You decide. Your seat is confirmed only after payment and our written confirmation.
