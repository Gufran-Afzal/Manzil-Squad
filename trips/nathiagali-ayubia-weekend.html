Overview

This is our first planned trip and it is still being put together. We are collecting registrations of interest so we know whether there is enough demand before we book transport and accommodation.

The plan is simple: leave Lahore early, reach the Galiyat by the afternoon, spend the evening around Nathiagali, walk the Pipeline Track the next morning and return to Lahore the same night. The pace suits first-time hill travellers and the walking is gentle.

Nothing below is final. Dates, the exact price, the coach company and the place we stay will be confirmed in writing before anyone is asked to pay.

The route

From about 217 m in Lahore to about 2,410 m in the hills. Distances are rounded road estimates and elevations are approximate town elevations. Check both before relying on them for planning.

Day by day

This is a draft outline. Exact timings, meal stops and the order of activities will be confirmed in the final itinerary.

Day 1Lahore to the Galiyat

Early morning

Depart Lahore

Meet at the pickup point. Coordinators check names against the confirmed list before boarding.

Midday

Motorway and rest stop

Travel north by motorway with a stop for food and prayers. Expect a long day on the road.

Afternoon

Arrive in the hills

Climb from the plains through Murree towards Nathiagali, then check in and rest.

Evening

Nathiagali walk

An easy walk around the Nathiagali bazaar and to St. Matthew's Church, a wooden church from the colonial period. Dinner and a group briefing for the next day.

Day 2Pipeline Track and the return

Morning

Pipeline Track

Walk the roughly 4 km forest track between Dunga Gali and Ayubia, inside Ayubia National Park. Mostly level, shaded and suitable for beginners.

Late morning

Time in Ayubia

Free time in Ayubia. The chairlift is optional and only if it is operating that day.

Afternoon

Head back south

Lunch, then board the coach for the return journey.

Night

Arrive in Lahore

Drop-off at the original pickup point. The arrival time depends on traffic and weather.

What to bring

  • A warm jacket, even in summer: evenings in the hills are much colder than Lahore
  • Comfortable walking shoes with grip
  • A light rain jacket during the monsoon months
  • Your original CNIC or student card, carried on you
  • Any personal medicines, with a copy of the prescription
  • A power bank and a small torch
  • A reusable water bottle

More detail in our Northern Pakistan packing checklist.

Safety

  • Coordinators will keep a headcount at every stop and a written list of emergency contacts.
  • We will check weather and road advisories before departure and will postpone if conditions are unsafe.
  • Stay with the group on forest trails. Wildlife, including rhesus macaques, lives in Ayubia National Park: do not feed animals.
  • Tell the coordinators privately, before the trip, about anything that could affect your safety on a walk.

Read what we do and what we ask of participants on the safety page.

Questions about this trip

Is this trip confirmed?

Not yet. It is a proposed trip. We will only confirm it once enough people have registered interest and we have confirmed transport and accommodation.

How hard is the walking?

The Pipeline Track is about 4 km and mostly level. If you can walk for an hour at an easy pace, you should be comfortable.

Can I come if I'm not a UOL student?

Yes. The trip is planned for students from any university. You may be asked to show a valid student card.
