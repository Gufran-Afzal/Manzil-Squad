Who we are

This website is run by [Brand Name], a student travel project based in Lahore, Pakistan. You can contact us about privacy using the details on the contact page.

What we collect

When you register interest in a trip, we ask for:

  • your full name
  • your mobile number
  • your email address, if you choose to give it
  • your university
  • how many people the request is for
  • how you would like us to contact you
  • any notes you choose to add
  • the date and time you agreed to this policy

When you send a message through the contact form, we ask for your name, an email address or mobile number, the topic, and your message.

We deliberately do not ask for CNIC numbers or images, bank or card details, passwords, or medical information through this website. Please don't include them in the notes or message fields. If a trip needs any of these (for example, a hotel asking for CNIC details at check-in), we will explain why and ask privately, and we will collect only what is needed.

Why we collect it

We use your details only to:

  • contact you about the trip you asked about
  • tell you if the trip goes ahead, changes, is postponed or is cancelled
  • plan numbers for transport and accommodation
  • reply to your message

We do not sell your information, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list without asking.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are stored in a Google Sheet owned by [Brand Name], through a Google Apps Script. Only the people who organise trips can open it. Google processes this data as our service provider, under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

If you contact us on WhatsApp, your messages are handled by WhatsApp under its own terms.

How long we keep it

We keep registration details for [12 months] after the trip they relate to, unless we need them longer to deal with a payment, refund or complaint. Messages are kept for [12 months]. After that, we delete them.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies. [If analytics is switched on in the site settings, describe the provider here and what it records before enabling it.]

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Contact us using the details on the contact page and include your reference number if you have one.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
